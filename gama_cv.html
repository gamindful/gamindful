I am Gama
A PhD in Neuroscience who became passionate about the relationship between human and man-made brains, which led me to feel interested in Computer and Data Science. As a researcher, I developed a solid basis in developing workflows that start by organization of databases, data-driven analysis and explainable results, with visualizations and reports demonstrating the link between all those processes.
My work

The publication of several products resulting from the positions I have held, reflect the capability of both my work and my ideas to be developed and shared through manuscripts at any level, included high-level stakeholders.
